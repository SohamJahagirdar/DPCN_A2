Read the measures
Cross-field share is the number of distinct, known-field direct citers whose primary field differs from the seed’s retrieved primary field, divided by all known-field direct citers. An unknown field is never counted as in-field.
Raw field richness grows with citation volume. Exact rarefied richness at ten known citers is available only when at least ten are classified. It addresses sample size, not indexing bias.
Equal-age comparisons cover the publication year and three following calendar years. Recent incomplete windows are right-censored. Same-year order is indeterminate; year inversions remain flagged.
A complete query means its OpenAlex Core pagination was exhausted. It does not establish complete coverage of world scholarship. G2 is sampled, with unvisited branches explicitly unknown.
